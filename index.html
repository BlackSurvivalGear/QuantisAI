Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the QuantisAI Partner Programme.

To apply, click the "Become a Partner" button to open the Coming Soon modal. Once our full Partner Portal launches, you can complete a registration form detailing your industry presence. Our operations desk reviews applications within 24 hours to set up your partner credentials.
Every partner starts at the Bronze level. As soon as you refer a specified threshold of active subscribers (e.g., 25 for Silver and 50 for Gold), our automated promotion engine upgrades your profile. Upgraded tiers pay higher commissions instantly for the subsequent billing periods.
Commissions are compiled on the last calendar day of each month and distributed within 15 days of the following month (Net-15 terms). Payments can be wire-transferred directly to your designated bank account or processed via secure payment merchants.
Once authorized, you receive a unique affiliate link. When users click this link, a cookie is saved to their browser for 90 days. If they register or purchase any subscription plan within this period, their account is permanently mapped to your dashboard.
You don't need to manually apply for upgrades! Once the Partner Portal launches, our system scans active referred subscription statistics daily. Your tier will automatically transition, and a confirmation email notification will be triggered.
Unlike the Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers which have a 12-month commission limit per referral, Premium Partners earn commissions for as long as their referred customers remain subscribed. This elite tier is reserve-invited for enterprise affiliates, universities, and national representatives.
